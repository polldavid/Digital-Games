Privacy

Alaga is in beta. This page says plainly what happens to the information you put in it. Last updated 7 October 2026.

The short version: your baby’s log stays on your phone. There is no account, no sign-up, no ads and no tracking. By default the app sends one anonymous count a day — “Alaga was used today”, with no ID and nothing about your baby — and you can turn that off. Nothing else leaves your phone unless you turn on one of the optional features below.

What’s stored, and where

  • Everything you log — feeds, diapers, sleep, milk, medicine, temperatures, growth, health records and settings — is saved in your phone’s storage for this app (the browser’s storage for the web version, the app’s own storage for the Android app).
  • Photos you add (prescriptions, documents) are kept on your phone, made smaller first. They are never uploaded.
  • Removing the app, or clearing this site’s data in your browser, deletes it. Settings → Erase everything deletes it too.

The anonymous usage count (on unless you turn it off)

So we can tell how many families use Alaga, each phone sends at most one tiny message a day to our server. It contains only:

  • that Alaga was used today, and whether this is the phone’s first count this week, this month, or ever;
  • the app version, the kind of phone (web, iPhone, Android), and whether sharing is on.

There is no ID or device fingerprint, nothing from your log, and no names. The server only adds the messages up into daily totals by country — Cloudflare tells it the country from the connection, and your internet address is not stored. Turn it off any time: Settings → Your data → Anonymous usage count.

Optional features that use the internet

  • Sharing with a partner (off until you set it up). Entries are encrypted on your phone before they’re sent, with a key that exists only in the invite code or link — the sync server can’t read your log. The server stores encrypted records under scrambled names, plus when each was saved. Like any website, it also sees your phone’s internet address while syncing. The server runs on Cloudflare. To remove the server copy: Settings → Share with a partner → Delete the shared copy. “Stop sharing” on one phone does not delete it.
  • Voice logging (off until you turn it on). Speech is turned into text by your phone’s speech service — Google on Android, Apple on iPhone — the same as voice typing, under their privacy terms. Alaga only receives the words, and they stay on your phone.
  • Reading a prescription photo. The text reader is downloaded the first time you use it; the photo itself is read on your phone and never uploaded.
  • Invite QR code. The QR-code maker is downloaded when you show an invite; the code itself is made on your phone.

The app’s own files are served by Cloudflare, which keeps standard short-lived request logs (internet address, time, page requested), as any web host does. Alaga adds no cookies or trackers.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts, emails or phone numbers collected.
  • No advertising, no selling or sharing of data, no tracking — only the anonymous daily count above, which you can switch off.
  • No one at Alaga can see your log — not even with sharing on.

Health information

Alaga gives general information based on AAP, CDC, WHO and NHS guidance. It is not medical advice and doesn’t replace your pediatrician. It never suggests medicine doses. If you’re worried about your baby, call your pediatrician — or emergency services if it’s urgent.

Questions

This is a beta shared with a small group of parents. If you have questions about privacy or want help deleting your data, contact the person who invited you to test.
