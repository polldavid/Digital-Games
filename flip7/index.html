Rules & scoring

The short version — enough to settle an argument mid-round.

  1. 1
    Flip or stay. On your turn take another card, or stay and bank what you've got.
  2. 2
    A duplicate number busts you. Your whole round scores 0 — modifiers and all.
  3. 3
    Seven different numbers is a Flip 7. Worth +15, and the round ends there and then.
  4. 4
    First to 200 after a completed round wins. 🏆

How a round is counted

Number cardsadded up
×2doubles that total — the numbers only
÷2halves it instead, rounding down Vengeance
+2 … +10added after the doubling
−2 … −10taken off after it — a round can finish below zero Vengeance
Flip 7+15, added last
Bust0 — nothing else counts

Example: 5 + 9 + 10 with a ×2 and a +4 → (24 × 2) + 4 = 52.
With a −6 as well → 48 + 4 − 6 = 46.
