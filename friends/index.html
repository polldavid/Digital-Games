Party Game

Do You Really Know
Your Friends?

One phone. One friend in the spotlight. Everyone else guesses how they'll answer. Find out who really knows who.

Best with 3–8 friends • Pass-and-play • No sign-up

← All games

How to play

  1. 1
    Add your friends. Everyone plays on this one device, passing it around.
  2. 2
    Spotlight a player. Each round one friend is in the spotlight and secretly answers a question about themselves.
  3. 3
    Everyone guesses. One by one, the others guess what the spotlight player secretly wrote.
  4. 4
    Reveal & score. The spotlight player judges each guess. Every correct guess scores a point.
  5. 5
    Rotate. The spotlight passes on. Most points at the end wins bragging rights.
🔒
A “pass the phone” screen guards every secret step, so answers and guesses stay hidden until the big reveal.
