In-Between

How to play

Also known as Acey-Deucey or Between the Sheets. One deck, one pot, a lot of nerve.

  1. 1
    Everyone antes into the pot to start.
  2. 2
    Two cards are dealt face up — the posts. Aces are high, but if your first card is an Ace you call it low or high. A second Ace is always high.
  3. 3
    Bet anything from 1 chip up to the whole pot that the next card lands strictly between the posts. Or pass.
  4. 4
    The third card decides it. Between: take your bet from the pot. Outside: your bet goes into the pot.
  5. 5
    Hit the post — match either post's rank — and you pay double your bet into the pot.
  6. 6
    Clean out the pot and everyone antes again. Run out of chips and you're out. Last one with chips — or the biggest stack when you call it — wins. 🏆

Awkward hands

7 · 8Consecutive — nothing fits between, so the turn passes free
9 · 9A pair — bet the next card is higher or lower. Three of a kind costs triple
A · KCall the Ace low and you've got 11 ranks to hit. Call it high and it's consecutive
PostYou never pay more than you have — it takes the lot

Example: posts 4♣ and J♦, you bet 10. A 9♥ wins you 10. A K♠ loses 10. A J♥ hits the post — pay 20.

House rules (set them in setup)

  • First lap: straight after an ante, nobody may bet the whole pot.
  • Post penalty: classic double, or a gentler single.
  • Ante every lap: top up the pot each time play goes round, not only when it's emptied.
  • Pairs: higher-or-lower, or just pass the turn.
  • Odds: the app can show your chances, counted from the cards still in the deck.
  • Turn timer (online): dawdle past it and your turn passes, so nobody stalls the table.

Playing online

  • Start a room and share the link — everyone joins from their own phone or laptop.
  • The host sets the house rules, adds computer players, deals, and can skip anyone who wanders off.
  • The server deals, so no one can peek at the deck. Late arrivals can sit down mid-game.

Who's playing?

2–8 players around one phone. Add computer players to fill the table or play solo.
