How to play

Split into teams. On your turn, hold the phone and race the 30-second clock — every word your team gets is a point.

  1. 1
    Make teams. Two or more teams, gathered around one phone.
  2. 2
    Take your turn. A word appears — Sing, Act, or Explain it so your team can shout the answer.
  3. 3
    Tap ✓ for each one your team gets, or Skip to move on. Beat the clock!
  4. 4
    Pass the phone to the next team and repeat.
  5. 5
    Most points after all the rounds wins. 🏆
